Useful answers

Before you book.

A few straightforward answers to help you feel informed about your first step.

Ask the team a question →
Is Azure Dental accepting new private patients?

Yes. New patients can book online. If you are unsure which appointment to choose, call the team and they can help you find the right starting point.

Where is Azure Dental located?

The practice is at 6 The Beacons, 1 School Lane, Formby, L37 3LN, opposite Waitrose in Formby village.

Can the team help nervous patients?

Tell the team when booking. They can discuss your concerns, explain each stage and agree a pace and pauses that help you stay involved in decisions about your care.

Does Azure Dental offer urgent appointments?

Yes. If you have pain, swelling, a broken tooth or another urgent concern, call as early as possible so the team can explain current availability. Same-day appointments depend on clinical need and diary availability.

How much does a first appointment cost?

The published new-patient examination fee is £99.75. The booking portal requests a £99.75 deposit. Further treatment is discussed separately, with suitable options and fees explained before you decide.

Does Azure Dental provide NHS dentistry?

Azure Dental is a private dental practice. If you are looking for NHS care, use the official NHS dental service search to check current availability in your area.
