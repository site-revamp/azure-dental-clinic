Azure Dental Website Redesign
1. Website Overview
Azure Dental is an independent private dental practice based in Formby, Merseyside, providing general, cosmetic, restorative and specialist dental treatments.

The practice serves individuals and families across Formby and surrounding areas, including Liverpool, Southport, Crosby and Ormskirk.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning Azure Dental as a modern, approachable and patient-focused dental clinic. It highlights the practice's comprehensive treatments, personalised care, patient comfort and support for nervous patients.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Treatment Presentation and Service Organisation
- Reorganised the presentation of general, cosmetic, restorative and specialist dental services.
- Improved the visibility of key treatments, including Invisalign, composite bonding, dental implants, veneers and teeth whitening.
- Created a clearer distinction between routine dental care and advanced treatment options.
- Addresses the challenge of helping visitors navigate the clinic's extensive treatment offering.
2. Stronger Patient-Focused Messaging
- Refined the homepage communication to emphasise personalised treatment planning, patient comfort and approachable dental care.
- Strengthened the presentation of support for nervous patients.
- Highlighted the clinic's independent practice identity and comprehensive treatment offering.
- Addresses the need to communicate what makes Azure Dental different from a standard dental practice and build confidence among prospective patients.
3. Improved Patient Journey and Contact Accessibility
- Improved the organisation of homepage content to create a clearer progression from exploring treatments to contacting the practice.
- Strengthened the visibility of relevant appointment and contact information.
- Improved the presentation of the Formby location and surrounding service areas.
- Addresses the challenge of helping prospective patients understand where the clinic is located and how to take the next step towards treatment.
3. Design Improvements
The redesigned homepage introduces a more cohesive and professional appearance aligned with Azure Dental's modern, patient-focused positioning.

Key improvements include:
- Visual presentation: A refined appearance that balances professional dental care with a welcoming atmosphere.
- Layout: Better organisation of treatment sections, practice information and patient-focused content.
- Typography: Clearer heading hierarchy and improved readability.
- Consistency: More cohesive presentation of the clinic's services, messaging and identity.
- User experience: A more straightforward browsing journey for patients exploring treatments and appointment information.
- Content hierarchy: Greater emphasis on key treatments, patient reassurance and contact accessibility.

5. Project Objective
The objective of the homepage redesign is to strengthen Azure Dental's online presence and make its treatment offering easier for prospective patients to understand and explore.

The updated direction prioritises treatment clarity, patient reassurance, personalised care and contact accessibility while reinforcing the clinic's identity as an experienced, independent private dental practice in Formby.
